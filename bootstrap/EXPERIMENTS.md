# AI·NT — Experiment Convention

Experiments are evidence, not marketing.

For any material tournament or optimization, record at minimum:

- objective and hypothesis,
- baseline,
- candidate(s),
- fixed constraints and budget,
- benchmark/evaluation version,
- models/providers used,
- resource/inference consumption,
- quality and correction measures,
- security/adversarial findings where relevant,
- result,
- uncertainty/limitations,
- decision or next experiment.

The component being evaluated should not have unilateral authority over its benchmark or telemetry. Preserve enough raw evidence to reproduce or challenge a conclusion.
